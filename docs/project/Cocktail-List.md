# Phoenix - Cocktail Sheet

***Basic liquor choices: Whiskey, Tequila, Vodka, Gin, Rum, Cointreau***

> All steps ✅ — ready for Mixing Game.

---

### 1. Whiskey Sour (shake) ✅
- 45-60 mL Bourbon whiskey based on player needs
- 30 mL lemon juice
- 20-25 mL syrup (can change to osmanthus/elderflower/longa syrup based on player preference)

**Steps:**
1. Fill shaker with ice
2. Add bourbon, lemon juice, and syrup
3. Shake vigorously for 12-15 seconds
4. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice
5. Garnish with a lemon slice or cherry

---

### 2. Bloody Mary (shake) ✅
- 45 mL Vodka
- 90 mL Tomato juice
- 20 mL lemon juice

**Steps:**
1. Fill shaker with ice
2. Add vodka, tomato juice, and lemon juice
3. Shake briefly — 5-8 seconds only, do not over-shake
4. Strain into a tall glass over ice
5. Garnish with a celery stick or lemon wedge

---

### 3. Gin Tonic (stir) ✅
- 30 mL Gin
- 10-20 mL lemon juice based on user preference
- Add tonic water to fill the whole cup

**Steps:**
1. Fill a tall glass with ice
2. Pour gin over ice
3. Add lemon juice
4. Top with tonic water
5. Stir gently once — do not over-stir or the carbonation dies

---

### 4. Tom Collins (shake) ✅
- 45 mL Gin
- 30 mL lemon juice
- 15-20 mL syrup based on user preference

**Steps:**
1. Combine gin, lemon juice, and syrup in a shaker with ice
2. Shake for 10 seconds
3. Strain into a tall glass over fresh ice
4. Top with soda water
5. Stir gently once and garnish with a lemon slice

---

### 5. Iced Lychee (shake) ✅
- 45 mL Gin
- 5-6 pieces of lychee
- 30 mL lychee juice

**Steps:**
1. Muddle 5-6 lychee pieces in the shaker
2. Add gin and lychee juice
3. Fill with ice and shake until the shaker frosts
4. Strain into a glass over a large ice cube

### 17. Mojito (build) ✅
- 50 mL Rum
- 20 mL syrup
- 20 mL lime juice
- Fill with soda
- Add mint leaves

**Steps:**
1. Add lime juice and syrup to the glass
2. Add mint leaves and gently press with a muddler — press, don't crush
3. Fill glass with ice
4. Pour rum over ice
5. Top with soda water and stir gently from the bottom up
